## Overview

Moto Service Log is a motorcycle maintenance tracking product concept designed for riders who want to manage service history, parts replacement, mileage, and ownership records in a more organized way.

The idea came from a real rider problem: maintenance information is often scattered across memory, notes, receipts, chats, and workshop visits.

This product concept focuses on creating a clean and practical workspace for tracking motorcycle ownership over time.

## Problem

Motorcycle maintenance is easy to forget and hard to track consistently.

Many riders rely on memory or manual notes to remember when they last changed oil, replaced tires, checked brake pads, serviced the chain, or installed new parts.

This can create problems such as:

- missed maintenance schedules
- unclear service history
- forgotten part replacement dates
- no centralized record for ownership costs
- difficulty sharing service history when selling a motorcycle
- lack of visibility into long-term maintenance patterns

## Goal

The goal is to design a practical maintenance log system for motorcycle owners.

The product should help riders:

- track service history by mileage and date
- log parts replacement
- manage maintenance schedules
- record ownership costs
- organize motorcycle data in one place
- share selected service history when needed

The focus is not only on logging data, but also making maintenance easier to understand and act on.

## Core Features

- Motorcycle profile management
- Service history log
- Mileage-based maintenance tracking
- Parts replacement records
- Custom replacement interval settings
- Parts library concept
- Cost tracking with optional privacy control
- Trip and route planning concept
- Shareable service history concept
- Responsive dashboard layout

## Architecture

Moto Service Log is still in product concept stage, but the intended architecture is designed around clear separation between data, UI, and product workflows.

Planned frontend structure:

- dashboard overview for each motorcycle
- service log module
- parts library module
- maintenance schedule module
- trip planning module
- settings and preferences
- reusable UI components
- responsive layouts for desktop and mobile

The architecture would prioritize simple but scalable modules so the product can grow without becoming hard to maintain.

## UX Decisions

The UX should feel useful for real riders, not like a generic CRUD app.

Important UX decisions:

- show the most important maintenance reminders first
- make service logging fast and simple
- separate motorcycle profile data from service records
- support mileage-based and date-based reminders
- make parts history easy to scan
- allow users to hide sensitive price information when sharing
- design mobile views carefully because riders may update logs from a phone

The product should feel like a personal garage dashboard.

## Challenges

The main challenge is balancing detail with usability.

Maintenance tracking can become too complex if every field is required. The product needs to support detailed records while still making quick logging easy.

Key challenges:

- designing flexible service categories
- handling different maintenance intervals per part
- making mileage-based reminders easy to understand
- keeping the logging flow simple
- designing useful mobile interactions
- avoiding over-engineering before validating the core workflow

## Result

Moto Service Log is currently a product concept that expands my portfolio beyond dashboard UI into product thinking and real-world workflow design.

It shows how I approach a problem from daily life, turn it into a structured product idea, and think through user flows, data structure, and frontend experience.

The concept can later be developed into a web app or mobile-first product for motorcycle riders.
